Raccoon Crystal Rugby

Grab it. Steal it. Guard it.

Raccoon Crystal Rugby is a frantic 30-second team game. Two teams race for two glowing crystals, carry them home to their glowing tray, and raid the other side's base to steal them back.

Every delivery scores a point, and every crystal still in your base when the clock hits zero is worth 5 more. Play 1 v 1 or 2 v 2 on phones or computers.

How to play
  1. Collect: run into a crystal to pick it up. You can carry one at a time.
  2. Steal: touch an opponent who's carrying one to take it instantly.
  3. Deliver: carry it into your team's glowing tray: +1 point and it turns your colour.
  4. Raid: crystals in a base aren't safe. Touch one in the other team's base to grab it.
  5. Pass: bump into your teammate to hand over your crystal.

When the 30 seconds are up, every crystal in your base is worth +5. Delivery points are never lost.
